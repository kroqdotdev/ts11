# Code of conduct

teamsheet11 follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and don't harass anyone.
Many people here volunteer at their club on top of everything else; be patient with questions.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue,
or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in
confidence.
